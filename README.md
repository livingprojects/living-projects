Living Projects
Carries your thinking from one session to the next.

Living Projects is a plugin. You add it to the AI you already use — Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. With it, your AI keeps the understanding — what you decided, why you decided it, what you tried that didn't work, and where things stand. It goes into plain files in your own storage, as you work. Any new chat reads them and continues. Nothing to re-explain, whether the last one was this morning or last year. Living Projects changes one thing — your information arrives complete, from one conversation to the next.

What it keeps
A record is a small set of plain .txt files inside a folder you choose:

Decisions — what was settled, and the reason it was settled that way
Rejections — paths that were tried and killed, and why they died
Definitions — words pinned to the meaning you gave them, in your words
Corrections — one saved fact replaced by another, and why it was wrong
Raw source — long accounts kept whole, word for word, nothing summarised
A parking lot — ideas with no home yet
A current standing — the live state, rewritten fresh each time
Nothing in the record is edited or deleted. A wrong entry is superseded by a later one and the history stays.

Where it lives
In your own storage — Dropbox, Google Drive, or Microsoft OneDrive. You approve the connection once, on your provider's own screen, and you can revoke it there at any time.

No account to create. No password. No files held anywhere else.

What the connector can and cannot do
It can build a project folder, list your projects, read a record, read a file, save a note, capture an entry, keep a raw source, supersede an old entry, prepare and save a standing, attach an additional storage, name the platform it is talking to, and update the project's home file on your yes.

It cannot delete anything, anywhere. It cannot edit record history. It cannot reach outside the project folder you point it at. Every write is verified in storage before it is reported as saved.

Getting started
Install the plugin.
Ask for a Living Projects home in the storage you use — for example, "create a living project in google drive". You will be handed one link to approve on your provider's screen.
Work normally. What settles gets saved as it settles.
When you want the state rewritten, say "organize".
