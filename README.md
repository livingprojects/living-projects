# Living Projects

**A free plugin for anyone tired of AI amnesia.**

Living Projects keeps your project's progress, decisions, and notes in your own
storage. A new chat reads them and picks up where you left off — no
copy-pasting, no re-explaining.

→ **[livingprojects.net](https://livingprojects.net)**

## Where it works

- **AI:** Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini
- **Storage:** Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive

Claude calls it a connector. ChatGPT calls it a plugin. Gemini calls it a custom app.

## Connect it

The address:

```
https://connect.livingprojects.net/mcp
```

| AI | Add it as | Where to find it | Plan |
|---|---|---|---|
| Claude | a custom connector | Settings → Customize → Connectors | all plans, free included |
| ChatGPT | a plugin | Settings → Integrations → Plugins | a paid plan |
| Gemini | a custom app | More → Customize → Connectors, then + Custom | Google AI Pro or Ultra |

Step-by-step for each AI: [livingprojects.net/connect](https://livingprojects.net/connect)

## What to type

Type these to your AI, in any chat.

1. **Start a project** — *"create a home using Living Projects and call it TRIP TO EUROPE"*
2. **Save a note** — *"… save this."*
3. **Continue in a new chat** — *"we're working in TRIP TO EUROPE home — where were we?"*

More ways to use it:

- *"fact — the hotel deposit is $500"*
- *"record this: we're taking the train to Lyon because it arrives in the city centre"*
- *"park this: I completely forgot Maya's birthday this week. get a gift."*
- *"save my exact words"*
- *"let's organize"*

All of them, with examples: [livingprojects.net/how-to-use-it](https://livingprojects.net/how-to-use-it)

## What's in a home

A home is your project's folder, in your own storage. Inside its Living Projects folder:

- **Notes** — what you save as you work, one folder per AI
- **The record** — decisions, options you chose not to pursue, what words mean to you, and corrections, each with its reason
- **Where it stands** — updated each time you organize
- **The Parking Lot** — ideas for later

Plain text files. Yours to keep, open and read.
